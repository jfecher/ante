Contributors welcome!

General guidelines:
- Look for the [good first issue tag](https://github.com/jfecher/ante/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20state%3Aopen%20label%3A%22good%20first%20issue%22) for issues which require minimal compiler-specific knowledge
- Feel free to break up issues into smaller issues as long as it leaves the compiler in a consistent state
- Commenting on issues you're working on is recommended to prevent others from potentially repeating the same work. Also feel free to comment asking for implementation tips for the issue.
- If interested in working on larger changes, please coordinate with me in the #contributing channel of [Ante's discord](https://discord.gg/BN97fKnEH2) or my email so I can provide tips and help get past any blockers.

## LLM Policy

- No AI-generated content is allowed in: PR descriptions, comments, or documentation.
- All code should be reviewed by a human before being submitted as a PR, and all relevant design decisions should be explained in the PR.
- PRs with AI-generated content are allowed, but entirely AI-generated PRs with low quality code may be closed without comment.
- Smaller PRs will have much more lenient reviews than larger PRs. Please do not submit several thousands of lines of entirely AI-generated changes.
